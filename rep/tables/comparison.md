## 4. Principal angles between sessions (mean angle, degrees; 155 channels active in all three)

The subspaces are estimated on all bins (PCA, FA) or on all windows (GPFA). Half – half: the two halves of the same session (trials 1-2, 5-6, … versus 3-4, 7-8, …), the noise floor. Three chance cases, all with subspaces drawn from a covariance as in Elsayed et al. 2016 (v = U S^1/2 r, then QR; 500 pairs): **all bins**, the covariance of the subspace data (flat spectrum, almost uniform, §4b); **condition means**, the covariance of the means of the 16 conditions (8 phases × odd/even version, 1 s windows from the start of the phase) with the preprocessing of the method, the chance case of Elsayed et al.; **uniform**, identity covariance. For pairs of sessions the chance case uses the mean of the two covariances, for the two halves the covariance of the session. Each chance case: median and, in parentheses, 5th percentile of the mean angle.
